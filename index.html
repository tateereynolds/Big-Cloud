HONEST BY DESIGN

A guide, not a grade.

Focus Studio estimates attention from eye position and head direction. It compares your current view with your calibrated screen and notes positions. It cannot measure comprehension or prove that you’re studying.

Focused: your face matches a calibrated viewing position, or you choose manual studying. Distracted: a phone is visible, your view stays outside both positions, or you choose manual distracted. Unobserved: the camera is off, the view is uncertain, this tab is hidden, or the computer sleeps.

A webcam cannot reliably distinguish reading notes from a phone held out of view. Glasses, lighting, posture, and camera angle affect accuracy. Brief blinks and glances are smoothed. Tune the viewing tolerance or use manual tracking when needed.

Observed focus rate is focused ÷ (focused + distracted). Coverage tells you how much study time was observed. Breaks and pauses never count as study time.

Your camera is optional and starts only when you enable it. Images are processed in your browser; the Python server receives only session totals. Video and audio are never recorded. Model libraries download from jsDelivr and Google when first enabled; internet is required for camera models.

Keep this local app on your own computer. It has no accounts or shared-user isolation. Your journal lives in a local SQLite file; calibration stays in this browser.
